Portfolio Risk ​

Portfolio-level risk contribution utilities.

RiskPerf.relative_risk_contribution Method
julia
relative_risk_contribution(weights, covariance_matrix)

Formula

The relative risk contribution (RRC) of an asset i is defined as the ratio of the asset's risk contribution RC_i to the total portfolio risk σ(w):

RRCi=RCiσ(w)=wi(Σw)iwTΣw

such that ∑i=1NRRCi=1.

Arguments

  • weights: Vector of asset weights in portfolio.

  • covariance_matrix: Covariance matrix of asset returns.
